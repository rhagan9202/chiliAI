# Policy POL-2024-IMAGING-003 — Prior Authorization for Advanced Imaging

**SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT — NOT A REAL CMS POLICY. Fictional IDs and providers.**

- **policy_id:** POL-2024-IMAGING-003
- **Effective:** 2024-04-01
- **Specialty:** Radiology

## Scope

This policy governs advanced diagnostic imaging and requires prior
authorization for CPT codes **70551**, **70552**, and **70553** (MRI of the
brain). Claims without an approved authorization number must be returned to
the provider.

Prior-authorization requests must be submitted by a provider with a Radiology
taxonomy. NPI 1122334455 (Radiology) is the example reviewing provider.

## Citations

This policy cites regulation section **42 CFR 410.32** for diagnostic ordering
requirements and **42 CFR 414.40** for fee-schedule determination.
