## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Which procedure codes are covered for telehealth E/M visits?**

A: This policy governs telehealth E/M and applies to CPT codes **99213** and
**99214** when furnished via interactive audio-video.

**Q: Who may bill these codes?**

A: A provider with a Family Medicine taxonomy. NPI 3344556677 (Family
Medicine) is the example billing provider.

**Q: What regulation supports telehealth coverage?**

A: This policy cites regulation section **42 CFR 410.78**, which defines the
conditions for Medicare telehealth services.
