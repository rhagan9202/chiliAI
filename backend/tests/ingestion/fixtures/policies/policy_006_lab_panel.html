Policy POL-2024-LAB-006 — Comprehensive Metabolic Panel Coverage

  • policy_id: POL-2024-LAB-006
  • Effective: 2024-02-01
  • Specialty: Clinical Laboratory

Scope

This policy governs laboratory panel billing and applies to CPT codes 80053 (comprehensive metabolic panel) and 80048 (basic metabolic panel). Panels must not be unbundled into individual component tests.

Claims are billed by a provider with a Clinical Laboratory taxonomy. NPI 4455667788 (Clinical Laboratory) is the example billing provider.

Citations

This policy cites regulation section 42 CFR 410.32 for diagnostic-test ordering requirements.
