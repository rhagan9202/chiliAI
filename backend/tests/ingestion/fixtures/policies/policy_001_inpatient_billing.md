## Scope

This policy governs initial hospital inpatient evaluation-and-management (E/M)
services. It applies to CPT codes **99221**, **99222**, and **99223**.

Inpatient E/M claims must be submitted by an attending physician with an NPI
on file. The maximum reimbursement amount is $250.00 per claim.
