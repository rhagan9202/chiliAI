Policy POL-2024-CARDIO-007 — Cardiac Stress Testing Coverage

  • policy_id: POL-2024-CARDIO-007
  • Effective: 2024-07-01
  • Specialty: Cardiology

Scope

This policy governs cardiac stress testing and applies to CPT codes 93015 and 93017. Coverage requires documentation of cardiovascular symptoms.

Claims are billed by a provider with a Cardiology taxonomy. NPI 5566778899 (Cardiology) is the example billing provider.

Citations

This policy cites regulation section 42 CFR 410.32 and supersedes prior policy POL-2022-CARDIO-003.
