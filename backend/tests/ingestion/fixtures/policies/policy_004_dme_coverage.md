## Scope

This policy governs coverage for durable medical equipment (DME). It applies
to HCPCS codes **E0143** (walker, folding, wheeled) and **E0260** (hospital
bed, semi-electric). Reimbursement is capped at the regional fee-schedule
amount.

DME suppliers must hold a valid Medicare supplier number. Provider NPI
2233445566 (DME Supplier) is the example billing supplier.
