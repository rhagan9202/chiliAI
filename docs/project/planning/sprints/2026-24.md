## Sprint goal

Make Policy Intelligence a real, durable, KB-scoped surface and eliminate seeded fixtures from the running product. By end of sprint: the worker generates policy review items from **domain-configured rule packs**, analysts triage them (accept/reject/defer/**escalate-to-case**) with persisted disposition, and the existing Policy page is reconciled onto that real contract; every remaining `ApiState._seed_*` source (conversations, workflows, alerts, policy gaps, the vestigial cases seed) is replaced by durable repositories/services; and records ingestion rejects duplicate **submissions** (not just duplicate record IDs) and malformed payloads with clear semantics. This closes the v1 surface set so the only remaining v1 work is config-write (BL-016) and ingestion UX (BL-013/014).

## Risks

- **R-01 — BL-011 reconciliation risk (primary).** The backlog treated BL-011 as greenfield, but a real-but-seeded policy surface already exists. Evolving `/policy/gaps` into `/policy/items` + triage (D-RECONCILE) plus config rule packs plus worker generation may exceed 8 SP. **Mitigation:** freeze the item+triage contract by mid-week 1 (pre-sprint design note); if reconciliation balloons, keep the existing `/policy/gaps` route name and add triage + worker-backed durability *under* it (contract-compatible) rather than renaming, deferring the rename to 2026-25. Do **not** pull the BL-028 stretch unless BL-011 is on track at the mid-week checkpoint.
- **R-02 — BL-012 alerts/policy-gaps coupling.** `_seed_alerts` and `_seed_policy_gaps` couple to the monitoring worker and BL-011 respectively; removing them naïvely can blank the Alert Feed / Policy page. **Mitigation:** remove the independent seeds (`conversations`, `workflows`) first; remove `alerts` only after confirming the monitoring-generated durable read path; `policy_gaps` removal is sequenced under BL-011, not BL-012.
- **R-03 — BL-015 over-build.** "Submission-level dedup" can expand into full ingest-ledger/audit territory. **Mitigation:** v1 = a submission idempotency key (or aggregate batch hash) + a typed reject result; defer reprocessing/audit-trail workflows (REQ-REC-003 advanced) to a post-v1 records story.
- **R-04 — Capacity & calendar drift.** 2026-23 merged 2026-06-02, **before** its nominal 06-09 start — work is landing far ahead of the stated windows, so both the 25 SP number and the 06-23→07-06 dates are nominal planning values, not observed throughput. **Mitigation:** at kickoff, compute actual 2026-22/23 throughput from merge dates and re-baseline capacity + the sprint calendar before committing engineers (carry-forward of the 2026-23 followup #1).
