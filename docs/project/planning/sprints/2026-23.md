## Sprint goal

Complete the **alert → evidence → case** investigative flow. By end of sprint: alerts carry real (non-seeded) evidence packs generated from graph context; analysts can view the evidence pack (subgraph + metrics + reasoning) in the Alert Feed and Investigation Workbench; and analysts can promote an alert into a durable, KB-scoped investigation case via a new Case Management surface. This sets up BL-011 Policy Intelligence (its `escalate-to-case` action depends on Cases) for 2026-24.

## Risks

- **R-01 — Critical-path concentration on BL-005.** BL-006 (3 SP) and BL-010 (8 SP) both depend on the `EvidencePack` shape. Mitigation: freeze the `EvidencePack`/builder contract by mid-week 1 (see pre-sprint prerequisite) so dependents integrate against a stable interface; BL-010's repository/endpoint scaffolding proceeds in parallel without it.
- **R-02 — BL-010 completion risk.** 8 SP spanning a Postgres migration, repository, four endpoints, a frontend page, and an e2e flow is the largest single item and the most likely to spill. Mitigation: the 4 SP buffer is reserved primarily for BL-010; if week-2 burndown lags, descope the `CaseManagementPage` filter/edit polish (keep list + promote-from-alert + status update) to a 2026-24 follow-on rather than dropping the durable repository.
- **R-03 — Evidence-pack traversal scope creep.** "relationship traversal + metric snapshot" can expand indefinitely. Mitigation: time-box BL-005 traversal to a fixed depth/seed-set for v1 (documented), defer ranking/pruning sophistication to a post-v1 evidence-pack story (see requirements §6 "Advanced evidence pack workflows" out-of-scope note).
- **R-04 — Capacity (20 SP) still a planning assumption.** Confirmed as the working number with the user, but not yet validated against actual 2026-22 throughput (that sprint ran on PM-defaulted capacity). Mitigation: review 2026-22 actuals at this sprint's kickoff and re-balance before committing engineers.
